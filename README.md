# Alex Eyre

**Biomedical informatics · Functional genomics · Research software**

I am a doctoral student in Biomedical Informatics at The Ohio State University and a Computational Analyst at Battelle. I develop computational approaches for organizing biological evidence, investigating complex traits, and supporting scientific discovery.

### Current research: DeathMap-AI

My dissertation project, **DeathMap-AI**, explores how integrating CRISPR evidence can help clarify cancer–immune interactions and identify potential immunotherapy targets.

I am currently developing automated workflows to discover and organize publications, screening metadata, and public dataset records. These workflows preserve source evidence, experimental context, and uncertainty to support reproducible analysis.

Longer-term research questions include:

- Which therapeutic targets are shared across cancer types?
- How do evidence scale and diversity influence target discovery?
- When might gene-network structures offer better intervention points than individual genes?

Planned work combines cross-dataset analysis with AI-assisted evidence synthesis to generate and prioritize testable hypotheses.

### Selected public work

**[DeathMap-AI: CRISPR evidence discovery](https://github.com/AWEyre7147/deathmap-ai)**  
A developing metadata discovery and curation pipeline for CRISPR screens, preserving source evidence and experimental context to support cancer–immune research.

**[2024TSA: canine behavioral genetics](https://github.com/AWEyre7147/2024tsa-gwas-validation)**  
Computational notebooks for evaluating previously identified behavioral loci in an independent canine cohort, with within-breed GWAS and cross-breed comparisons.

**[Ligand–receptor reference databases](https://github.com/AWEyre7147/ligand-receptor-database)**  
Human and mouse ligand–receptor curation workflows that preserve source evidence, species distinctions, and complete complexes for downstream computational biology analyses.
### Research approach

I emphasize traceable evidence, reproducible workflows, and clear distinctions between source observations, analytical results, and scientific interpretation.

My research background spans cancer immunology, canine behavioral genetics, plant-associated microbiomes, and fungal genomics. My public work includes Python, R, and Jupyter-based analyses.
